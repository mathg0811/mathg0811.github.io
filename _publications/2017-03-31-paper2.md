A single-shot quantitative phase imaging method using a color-coded LED microscope (cLEDscope). Red, green, and blue LEDs illuminate the sample at different angles, and phase reconstruction is achieved through computation with a color image sensor. A correction scheme is introduced to address color leakage from non-ideal LED and sensor spectra, improving phase accuracy.
